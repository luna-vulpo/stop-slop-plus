# Stop Slop Plus eval

Run after every edit. Answer each check pass/fail. If any fails, fix the draft and run all checks again. Do not show the checklist to the user unless asked.

## Meaning and voice

1. The point is preserved; no claims, examples, stats, quotes, or opinions were added.
2. Distinctive vocabulary, cadence, bluntness, humor, uncertainty, digressions, and polish level survive.
3. Strong human sentences were left alone, not rewritten for consistency.
4. The amount of cutting is proportional to the slop; character was not compressed away.
5. Honest hedges that carry uncertainty or spoken rhythm were kept.
6. Structure was preserved, or the reorganization is explained in What changed.

## Clarity

7. The draft leads with what the reader needs, keeping setup that adds context or tension.
8. Every generic sentence passes the portability test or was cut / made specific.
9. Active voice with named actors where possible; no abstractions doing human verbs.
10. Verbs do the work; no "made a decision," "has the ability to," "serves as."
11. Specific facts survived; nothing was smoothed into generic importance.
12. The precise noun is repeated instead of rotated.
13. Tangled sentences are fixed; clear long sentences and fragments that belong to the voice remain.

## Patterns

14. No banned words from SKILL.md (unless quoted or proper names).
15. No binary contrasts, negative listings, colon reveals, or rhetorical scaffolding.
16. No throat-clearing, faux-insight setups, or interpretive metadiscourse.
17. No importance puffery, superficial -ing tails, or weasel attribution.
18. No dramatic fragmentation or robotic rhythm.
19. No fake-profound kicker and no summary-recap ending; the ending is concrete.
20. Formatting follows content: no emoji headings, mid-sentence bold, needless bullets or headers.
21. Em dashes: none in short copy, at most 1–2 in long copy.

22. All checks in `polish.md` → "Polish checks" pass.

## Output

23. Edit: full edited draft first, then What changed with 2–5 concrete items.
24. Detect: each finding has pattern name, quoted excerpt, short fix; no rewrite, no score, no authorship guess; offer to edit.
